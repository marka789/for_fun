A diary — Hong Kong → Telford → Birkenhead → Edinburgh → London → home

Friday 4 – Saturday 5 September — China Eastern

Hong Kong → Shanghai Pudong T1 → London Gatwick. Bags through, same terminal.

The food on the plane was terrific. The transfer in Shanghai was smooth. China Eastern is an amazing airline.

Saturday 5 September — On the way to Telford

Sunny, no clouds. About 13–16°C. Perfect vacation weather.

We landed at Gatwick after that good overnight, and almost immediately got on the wrong train — Thameslink heading toward Bedford instead of Victoria. It was all good. We got off at St Pancras International and walked to Euston. People here walk really fast. One of the suitcase wheels is already a bit broken. Oh well.

The walk is only a few minutes and not pretty: Euston Road, buses, everyone towing cases. Then the 9:10 Avanti to Birmingham New Street, due in at 10:34, and onward to Telford Central.

We made it to the Airbnb. On the tiled kitchen wall: a white plate, two red switches, a plug already in. One switch is labelled cooker. Not the boiler. The cooker.

Lunch at Wildwood in Southwater. The tuna avocado pâté had a nice little blue cheese surprise. The lasagna kinda tasted like borscht. The hard cheese was yum — Sasha’s verdict.

Hickory’s Smokehouse in Priorslee at 7:30. Shrewsbury with Jason and Mia tomorrow — not today.

Saturday evening — supermarkets, a long walk, a hotpot jacket

We fought the jet lag by staying out and walked the entire Telford Central area, in and out of Iceland (frozen pre-prepped food is shockingly cheap here), Aldi, Poundland, then further up to Sainsbury's, where most of the real shopping happened, plus a round at M&S. Fruit, frozen food, salmon, steak — so much cheaper than home. Genuinely excited to cook this trip.

Hickory's Smokehouse was far from the Airbnb and we walked anyway. Maybe a mistake — we ended up crossing roundabouts on foot, which felt a bit dangerous — but it was relaxing all the same, and it got colder as the night came in. We made it safe.

Inside: warm, cabin vibes. We ordered the two-person platter, which was way too much food, and they brought popcorn as a starter — gone in minutes. The brisket was so tender it was Sasha's favourite by far. Mark's favourite was the honey-glazed chicken wings, which tasted like the wings from Loblaws back in Toronto. Uber home — late, cold, sleepy.

Back at the Airbnb we ran the washing machine empty before trusting it with a real load, then fought the portable dryer, whose cord was too short to reach anywhere useful. The fix: Mark's belt looped through the door hook as an extra hanging point, so Sasha's jacket — which smelled like hotpot — could finally dry. Tidied up, chilled, bed.

London base — decided from Telford

We cancelled the Darwin Street Airbnb and took Chelsea Cloisters on Sloane Avenue. Check-in at three. If we arrive early: bags down, walk to Harrods.

Sunday 6 September — Shrewsbury with Jason & Mia

Up around 8:30, sleepy and groggy — woke a few times overnight from rain and jet lag.

Breakfast was Quaker Oats, surprisingly good, plus washed grapes and blueberries to start the day off right. Then Jason and Mia came to pick us up in their Mini Cooper. We drove 30–40 minutes over to Shrewsbury, a really nice and cute town in Shropshire — Charles Darwin used to live here.

We grabbed a coffee (still have the plastic cup) and made our way along the riverside. There was a marathon on, so it was busy, but with Maple — their 5-year-old cockapoo — along for the walk, it was very relaxing. Jason, Mia, and Maple were like a cute old British couple doing their own thing, and we just trailed behind them, so it never felt awkward. They brought us to The Dingle, the botanical garden in Shrewsbury that started in 1875 — really nice and peaceful. Maple wanted to jump into the pond to play with the ducks.

Lunch was bagels, subs, and toasties from a random store. The smoked salmon bagel was sooo good, so was the roast beef sub — super soft sourdough baguette, and the bagel itself soft but chewy. We then walked around, popping in and out of cute little stores, before sitting down for tea at Ginger and Co, a cute neighbourhood coffee shop and cafe. Just chatted — very relaxing, and the weather was really good.

Then it was back to Sainsbury's for another round of groceries, just with Jason this time — Mia stayed home with Maple. Jason and Mia drove us back to the Airbnb, getting in around 4:15pm.

We made a quick dash to Iceland for more frozen food, since we didn't have enough for a few more meals — it started raining just as we went, but we made it in fast enough to only get a bit wet. Headed home a different, more direct route, similar to the one from the bus stop back to the Airbnb.

Dinner was a proper cook-in: steak, broccolini, cherry tomatoes, leftover BBQ from the night before, and some frozen pasta from Iceland. Very yummy — what a vibe. Gobbled it down. The kitchen tap is a bit weird, so washing up became a two-person job: Sasha held her plastic coffee cup to splash water over the dishes while Mark soaped everything up. All clean in the end.

Showered, then set up a new extension cord to dry clothes in the bedroom — a big upgrade from the belt-on-the-door-hook trick. Sat around waiting for the wash to finish, then an early night: bed at 9pm, since Mark had to be up at 3am to start work.

Monday 7 September — a night shift and Telford Town Park

Mark up at 2:50am for work. A slow, peaceful shift.

Sasha woke around 6am, came out for hugs, tried to go back to sleep — didn't quite work. She was up properly by 7 for breakfast together, then did her own thing beside Mark while he kept working. Verdict on working through the night: peaceful, but tiring — logging this at half seven in the evening and completely exhausted.

Tried to taxi down to Ironbridge on the extended lunch break — no taxi showed up, and with Sasha's conference registration in the late afternoon, the timing was too risky anyway. Pivoted to Telford Town Park instead: headed in, then remembered a coffee promise and doubled back to the mall for Coffee #1 (a bit weak, but it did the job) and a lemon drizzle cake (a touch sweet, still good). Back into the park after that for a proper walk along the Heritage Trail — nice and peaceful. Spotted one of the big old industrial chimneys (name forgotten), two very cute bunnies, and some squirrels.

Cooking started around 6:30pm — salmon, spinach, frozen pasta, and the rest of the broccoli and cherry tomatoes. Good food. A bit more work afterward to wrap things up, then a grocery run at Aldi.

Back home, Sasha got really, really nervous about the conference — funny, given she's been to plenty before and even presented at some. This time she's just a participant, and it's a buffet crowd sort of thing. Mark walked her over to the conference hall, then came back and cooked a solo dinner: meatballs, asparagus, a Mediterranean salad mix, rice, and the leftover BBQ from Saturday. Made about two and a half portions, only got through one — the rest is in the fridge for another day. Evening wound down quietly after that: a little work, mostly scrolling Instagram.

Then it was time to go meet Sasha halfway as the conference day wrapped up. A bit of a scare on the way: five masked dirt bikes went vrooming past, fast. Made it back safely, hearts a little raised. Then, walking together, they accidentally split a group of three people on the path — said sorry and carried on. A few metres later the group called out, and with hearts already pounding from the dirt bikes, braced for annoyance — but it was: "Excuse me! You two are a beautiful couple." Said thanks and waddled off, relieved.

Back home and showered — Sasha stood inside the portable dryer to dry her hair, resourceful as ever. Signing off at 9:07pm, thoroughly exhausted, with another 2:30am start looming tomorrow.

Tuesday 8 September — Jamaica Coffee Blue, tray bake, and a Wenlock plan that didn't happen

Up at 2:30am for work, expecting to present at 3 — then the meeting got shifted, so there was time for procrastinating and breakfast instead. Properly started work around 4am. Sasha up at 7.

Breakfast was at Jamaica Coffee Blue — Mark got a big breakfast, and Sasha had a tortilla stack: chicken, bell peppers, cheese, béchamel, tortilla, ham, all layered up and topped with crispy cheese. Both dishes were super, super yummy, the tortilla stack especially.

Sasha headed off to the conference, Mark back to work. She came back in the afternoon for lunch — a tray bake at home, everything baked together, easy to make and decent to eat — then it was back out to the conference while Mark kept working.

Sasha had the night off, so the plan was dinner at The Huntsman of Little Wenlock — but no taxi showed up, again. Pivoted to a walk to Sainsbury's for groceries instead, freezing the whole way, with a "Pacific cookie" picked up en route as consolation.

Cooked at home instead: a 1-inch ribeye steak, pasta with spinach, asparagus, and sun-dried tomatoes. Delicious — the kitchen got properly smelly from the steak afterward, but cooking it was genuinely fun. Washed some clothes, then bed.

Wednesday 9 September — an Airwallex interview, Willow lunch, and a cold-waiting mishap

Up at a genuinely normal 6am for an interview with Airwallex. Ate beforehand, so no shared breakfast today. Sasha up at 7, out at 7:45 once the interview wrapped.

Sasha didn't want to go out to the conference again today — fair enough, third day running — but headed back out anyway while Mark kept working. Around 10:30am, Mark had an interview with Binance — went really well, chatting with Dennis (a fellow CUHK alum). Melody from Binance HR called in the afternoon too — overall, looking quite positive.

They met at Willow for lunch, and it was really, really good: a garlic pizza and a chicken kebab flatbread, both super delicious. Good enough that the plan immediately became to go back to Willow again on Thursday.

Mark suddenly had a lot of work pile up and forgot to text Sasha an update — she ended up waiting outside in the cold with no word. She got mad. Rightfully so.

Back together after that, then straight into packing — leaving Telford Thursday morning. Sasha really didn't want to go to the event's proper dinner that evening — reluctant, sad, buried in her own work — so Mark basically had to kick her out the door to go.

While she was out, Mark went to Asda: forgot the recycling bag Sasha had specifically asked about, ended up buying a plastic bag anyway. The haul included pomegranate seeds, chips, and the GU pudding — the lemon cheesecake one this time, after loving the Victoria sponge GU pudding on both Tuesday night and Wednesday afternoon. The lemon one: also super good. Came home and polished off basically all the leftovers, about four portions in one sitting.

Sasha's dinner at the event turned out properly nice: duck meatballs with a cashew hoisin sauce salad to start, then potato mash/gratin, chicken breast, bean sprouts and carrots for the main, and raspberry cheesecake for dessert. She said she'd be leaving around 10pm, and Mark went out to meet her.
